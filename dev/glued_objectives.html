Glued Objectives

glue, glue_catalogue, and count_oracle_cps_in_box build higher-dimensional test problems with known critical-point sets by Cartesian-product gluing: for F(z) = f₁(x) + f₂(y), the stationary points factorize as CP(F) = CP(f₁) × CP(f₂) and the Hessian is block-diagonal, so the Morse index adds.

The construction, the worked examples, the choice of factor pairs, and the verified recall results (himmelblau² and camel² — recall = precision = 1.0 at every Morse index) are documented with the solver they certify:

→ Glued Objectives (Globtim docs)

For the docstrings of glue, glue_catalogue, and count_oracle_cps_in_box, see the API Reference. Tests: test/test_glued_objectives.jl (construction, oracle-CP gradient-zero check, sub-box counting) and test/test_glued_recovery.jl (CI recall regression).
